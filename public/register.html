Data Privacy & Sharing
JEEPNi Passenger Data Privacy & Data Sharing Agreement

This explains how JeepNi collects, uses, stores, and shares passenger data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its IRR.

1. Data We Collect
  1. Personal (you provide) Name, Email, Phone Number.
  2. Operational Real-time passenger location, connectivity status (online/offline).
  3. We do not collect sensitive personal info (addresses, passwords, financial data, stored messages) unless you volunteer it for support/verification.
2. Purpose of Data Collection
  1. Provide accurate real-time map location and routing.
  2. Enhance commuting experience and ensure system performance/security.
  3. Support academic research, system improvement, reports/certification, and peak-hour prediction.
  4. Your data is never sold, rented, or used for ads without consent.
3. Data Sharing
  1. Transportation cooperatives: Only for monitoring jeepney activity. Your name/phone/email are not shared without explicit consent.
  2. Universidad de Dagupan: Capstone documentation, evaluation, research/defense. Aggregated/anonymized unless you consent.
  3. Government agencies: Only when required for LTFRB compliance, legal obligations, or safety, and only upon lawful request.
  4. Third-party services: Minimal necessary info (e.g., mobile number for SMS alerts). All must comply with RA 10173.
4. Data Storage & Retention
  1. Securely stored in Firebase with encryption and access controls; only authorized JeepNi developers may access logs.
  2. Location history and trip logs kept only as long as needed for research and analytics.
5. Data Protection Measures
  1. End-to-end encryption, secure Firebase rules, role-based access control.
  2. Limits on what data can be collected/stored; compliance with RA 10173 and ethical research standards.
6. Consent
  1. You agree to collection/processing of personal and location data for routing, analytics, documentation.
  2. You agree to sharing non-personal/anonymized data for research and system improvement.
  3. You may withdraw consent or request assistance: JeepNi Data Protection Officer (DPO) at cutest.rica@email.com.
8. Amendments

JeepNi may revise this agreement when necessary; passengers will be notified of important updates.

9. Acceptance

By checking the consent box or continuing to use JeepNi, you acknowledge you have read, understood, and agreed to this Data Privacy & Data Sharing Agreement.
